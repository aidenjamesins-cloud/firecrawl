How to choose in five questions

  1. How often does your team see a doctor? Frequent visits favor the copay and major medical plans, where primary care and specialist visits are a flat fee.
  2. What's the worst year you could absorb? Compare out-of-pocket maximums, not just deductibles. Platinum caps individual costs at $1,250; the HSA 9000 at $10,100.
  3. Do you want a health savings account? Four plans are HSA-eligible: Ameristead HSA 6000 and 9000, Bright Life HSA 5500 and 8500. Ask your tax professional how contributions fit your situation.
  4. Does anyone take regular prescriptions? Generics are $0 on the Ameristead and Bright Life copay plans and $10 on Bright Life major medical. On the HSA plans, prescriptions apply after the deductible. GLP-1 rules differ by plan family.
  5. One plan or a choice? Many owners offer two: a low-deductible plan and an HSA plan, so each person picks what suits them.
