How to choose in five questions

  1. How often does your team see a doctor? Frequent visits favor the copay plans (Bronze through Diamond), where primary care and specialist visits are a flat fee.
  2. What's the worst year you could absorb? Compare out-of-pocket maximums, not just deductibles. Platinum caps individual costs at $1,250; the HSA 9000 at $10,100.
  3. Do you want a health savings account? Only the HSA 6000 and HSA 9000 are HSA-eligible. Ask your tax professional how contributions fit your situation.
  4. Does anyone take regular prescriptions? The copay plans use $0/$35/$75 tiers. On the HSA plans, prescriptions are 30% after the deductible. GLP-1s need preauthorization every 6 months, an A1C of 6 or more, and mail order.
  5. One plan or a choice? Many owners offer two: a low-deductible plan and an HSA plan, so each person picks what suits them.
