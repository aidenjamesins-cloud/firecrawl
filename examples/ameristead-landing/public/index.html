Questions business owners ask.

Do I need employees to get a plan?

No. Tell us it's just you in the quote form and we'll show you the options available to the self-employed in your state.

Can I keep my doctors?

If your doctors are in the Cigna PPO network, yes. You can also see out-of-network providers at a higher cost share. We'll check your doctors before you enroll.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No. None of the plans require a referral to see a specialist.

Which plans work with a health savings account?

Four of them: Ameristead HSA 6000 and HSA 9000 (70/30 after the deductible) and Bright Life HSA 5500 and HSA 8500 (80/20 after the deductible). All four include $0 in-network preventive care.

What's the difference between Ameristead and Bright Life?

Both use the Cigna PPO network. Ameristead Health offers seven plans including a $0-deductible Diamond plan. Bright Life offers copay, major medical and HSA plans with deductibles from $1,250 to $8,500. We compare both for your business and show you the best fits.

What does the quote cost?

Nothing. There's no fee and no obligation to enroll.

How are GLP-1 medications like Ozempic or Mounjaro covered?

It depends on the plan family. Ameristead plans require preauthorization every 6 months, an A1C of 6 or more, and mail order. Bright Life plans cover them through a GLP-1 rider for qualifying members, not for weight loss.
